# Lab 7 - Foundations Models and Generative Models
This lab explores the concepts and applications of foundation models, including transformers and large language models (LLMs), as well as generative models such as GANs and VAEs.

- 2. Generative IA: main concepts and types of models, including GANs, VAEs, and Diffusion Models. Usage of Hugging Face library for generative models. Hallucinations in generative models.
- 3. Generative IA in computer vision
- 4. Generative IA in NLP
- 5. Strategies of prompting and fine-tuning and last trends of language models
